# OPC Website Promo Video Design

## Source

Captured from `https://findopc.online/`.

## Visual Identity

The video should feel like a polished product walkthrough of the real website: clean white canvas, calm institutional data interface, subtle blue-gray technology accents, iOS-like frosted panels, and restrained motion.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F7F7F4`
- Paper: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#111827`
- Body text: `#474B45`
- Muted text: `#64748B`
- Accent blue: `#315CDB`
- Soft blue: `#EFF6FF`
- Border: `rgba(23, 25, 22, .09)`
- Strong border: `rgba(23, 25, 22, .16)`

## Typography

- Primary: `SF Pro Display`
- Data/metadata: `SF Mono`

Use large, quiet typography only when text appears inside the captured website. Do not add explanatory marketing copy over the video.

## Motion

- Slow camera pushes and pans on screenshots.
- Soft blur crossfades and light wipe transitions.
- Subtle line/dot motion inspired by the homepage network effect.
- No flashy glitch, no heavy dark mode, no fake product claims.

## What Not To Do

- Do not redefine OPC in the video.
- Do not add narration text, captions, or extra introduction copy.
- Do not use unrelated stock imagery.
- Do not cover the website screenshots with large labels.
- Do not change the website brand/title shown inside the captured pages.
