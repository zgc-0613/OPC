# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SoloFirm serves Chinese entrepreneurs, researchers, and policy analysts investigating one-person companies and AI-enabled entrepreneurship. They use the public interface to move quickly from a broad opportunity question to verifiable policies, cases, regions, source records, and original evidence.

## Product Purpose

SoloFirm is a public index for AI + OPC policies, entrepreneurial cases, regional coverage, and traceable sources. Success means a user can understand the landscape at a glance, narrow the dataset with predictable filters, open the relevant record, and return to the original source without losing trust in the data.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, rigorous, focused. The experience should feel editorial and visionary while remaining calm enough for repeated research work. Motion creates orientation and emphasis; it must never obscure data or slow down a task.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue or slate SaaS dashboards with interchangeable cards and neon network decoration.
- Marketing pages that replace useful data with slogans, decorative metrics, or empty visual spectacle.
- Visual redesigns that rename SoloFirm, redraw its existing mark, remove content, hide controls, or alter route/API semantics.
- Overactive motion, glass effects, gradient text, nested cards, and low-contrast cinematic styling.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve evidence and action: every existing route, filter, source link, state, and data relationship remains reachable.
2. Lead with one memorable frame: the SoloFirm hero establishes the cinematic identity, then yields to legible working surfaces.
3. Reveal complexity progressively: large editorial statements introduce each area, while dense information stays scan-friendly and task-oriented.
4. Make provenance visible: source traceability, status, dates, and counts should read as first-class product information.
5. Motion explains hierarchy: use restrained pull-up, fade, and scroll-linked reveals with complete reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic landmarks, accessible names, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, and strong text contrast. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep content visible without animation, avoid color-only state communication, and ensure Chinese and English labels remain legible across mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts.
